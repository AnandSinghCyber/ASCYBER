Finance & Accounting

The rigorous journey to mastering taxation, audits, and corporate finance.

Chartered Accountant (CA - India)

The ultimate qualification in accounting, dealing with complex financial audits, tax laws, and business strategy.

Step 1

Foundation Course

The entry-level exam.

  • Register with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) after Class 10 or 12.
  • Appear for the CA Foundation exam (covers Accounting, Business Laws, Quantitative Aptitude, and Business Economics).
  • Note: Graduates with a required minimum percentage can skip Foundation and directly enter Intermediate.
Step 2

Intermediate Course

The core syllabus.

  • Divided into two groups (Group I and Group II) consisting of various rigorous papers including Costing, Taxation, and Auditing.
  • You must clear either one or both groups to proceed to the practical training phase.
Step 3

Articleship (Practical Training)

The crucial real-world experience.

  • Undergo 2 to 3 years of mandatory practical training under a practicing Chartered Accountant.
  • This is where you learn how to actually conduct audits, file corporate taxes, and handle real client finances.
  • Complete mandatory IT and Soft Skills training (ICITSS).
Step 4

CA Final

The ultimate test.

  • Register for the CA Final course during the last phase of your Articleship.
  • Clear both groups of the CA Final examination (Advanced Auditing, Direct/Indirect Tax Laws, Financial Reporting, etc.).
  • Known for its exceptionally low pass percentage. Intense preparation is required.
Step 5

Membership & Practice

  • Upon clearing the CA Final and completing Articleship, enroll as a member of ICAI.
  • You can now officially use the 'CA' prefix and either join corporate finance roles, consulting firms (Big 4), or start your own independent practice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How hard is the CA exam?
It is considered one of the toughest professional courses in the world. The pass percentage for the CA Final often hovers around 10-15%. It requires immense discipline, long hours of study, and a deep conceptual understanding of accounting standards and constantly changing tax laws.
Does it cost a lot to become a CA?
Unlike medical or engineering degrees in private colleges, the official fees paid to ICAI for registration, exams, and materials are very low (around ₹50,000 to ₹75,000 for the entire course). The major cost is private coaching classes, but overall, it is an extremely cost-effective professional degree.
