Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a degree to get into Cybersecurity?
No, a degree is not strictly required. Experience, certifications (like CompTIA Security+, OSCP), and a strong portfolio (CTFs, bug bounties) are often valued much more than formal education in this industry. However, a degree in Computer Science can help you bypass HR filters.
What is the average salary?
In the US, an entry-level SOC Analyst usually makes between $65k - $85k. Experienced Penetration Testers and Security Engineers can easily make $120k - $200k+, depending on location and specialization. In India, entry-level salaries range from ₹4 LPA to ₹8 LPA, scaling significantly with experience.
How long does it take to learn?
If you are starting from zero (no IT knowledge), it typically takes 1-2 years of consistent, daily studying to grasp the fundamentals of networking, Linux, and security tools well enough to land an entry-level job.
