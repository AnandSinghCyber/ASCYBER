Career Roadmaps

Your step-by-step guide to achieving your dream career. From tech to medical and civil services.

IT & Cyber Medical (Doctor) Civil Engineering IAS / IPS Skilled Trades

Technology & Cybersecurity

Penetration Tester

The Offensive Security path. Learn to think like an attacker to uncover vulnerabilities before the bad guys do.

Step 1

IT Fundamentals

Before you can hack it, you need to understand how it works.

  • Networking Concepts (TCP/IP, OSI, DNS, HTTP)
  • Linux Basics & Command Line
  • Windows Active Directory Basics
Step 2

Scripting & Programming

Automate your tasks and understand exploits.

  • Python (Writing custom scripts)
  • Bash Scripting
  • Basic C/C++ (Reading exploits)
Step 3

Security Basics & Tools

Learn the tools of the trade.

  • Nmap (Network Scanning)
  • Metasploit Framework
  • Burp Suite (Web Proxies)

SOC Analyst

The Blue Team path. Defend organizations by monitoring networks, analyzing alerts, and responding to cyber incidents.

Step 1

Network & OS Mastery

  • Deep Networking (Ports, Protocols, Firewalls)
  • Windows Internals & Event Logs
  • Linux Administration
Step 2

Defensive Tooling

  • SIEM (Splunk, Elastic, Azure Sentinel)
  • EDR/XDR Solutions (CrowdStrike, Defender)
  • Intrusion Detection (Snort, Suricata)

Medical Sciences

Doctor (MBBS)

The path to becoming a medical professional in India, saving lives and providing healthcare.

Step 1

High School (10+2)

Build your foundational science knowledge.

  • Choose Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB)
  • Maintain excellent academic grades
  • Begin preparing for medical entrance exams early
Step 2

Entrance Examination

Clear the national level entrance exam to secure a medical seat.

  • Prepare for and clear the NEET-UG exam
  • Attend counseling and secure admission into a Medical College
Step 3

MBBS Degree (4.5 Years)

The core medical education.

  • Pre-Clinical (Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry)
  • Para-Clinical (Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology)
  • Clinical (Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics, Gynecology)
Step 4

Mandatory Internship (1 Year)

Hands-on clinical training.

  • Rotatory internship across different hospital departments
  • Gain practical patient-care experience
  • Receive medical registration from MCI/NMC
Step 5

Post-Graduation (MD/MS) - Optional

Specialize in a specific medical field.

  • Clear the NEET-PG exam
  • Complete a 3-year MD (Medicine) or MS (Surgery) degree
  • Further super-specialize (DM/MCh) if desired

Engineering

Civil Engineer

Design, build, and maintain the infrastructure of the modern world.

Step 1

Educational Foundation

High school and college education.

  • Choose Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) in 10+2
  • Clear engineering entrance exams (JEE Main/Advanced, State Exams)
  • Enroll in a B.Tech / B.E in Civil Engineering (4 years)
Step 2

Core Subjects & Skills

Master the fundamental theories and software tools.

  • Structural Analysis, Fluid Mechanics, Soil Mechanics
  • Learn AutoCAD (2D/3D drafting)
  • Learn STAAD.Pro / ETABS (Structural Design)
  • Learn Primavera / MS Project (Project Management)
Step 3

Internships & Practical Exposure

Get your boots on the ground.

  • Complete summer internships at construction sites
  • Understand surveying (Total Station, Auto Level)
  • Learn estimation and costing
Step 4

Career Entry

Start your professional journey.

  • Site Engineer (Execution and Management)
  • Design Engineer (Office-based structural design)
  • Clear GATE exam for PSU jobs or M.Tech

Government & Civil Services

IAS / IPS (UPSC)

Serve the nation through the elite Indian Administrative Service or Indian Police Service.

Step 1

Graduation & Eligibility

The basic requirement for the civil services.

  • Complete a Bachelor's degree in any discipline
  • Ensure you meet the age limit criteria (usually 21-32 years)
Step 2

Phase 1: Prelims Examination

The initial screening test.

  • General Studies Paper 1 (History, Geography, Polity, Economy)
  • CSAT Paper 2 (Aptitude, Reasoning, Comprehension)
  • Develop a strong daily newspaper reading habit (The Hindu / Indian Express)
Step 3

Phase 2: Mains Examination

The subjective, in-depth written examination.

  • Essay Paper
  • 4 General Studies Papers (GS I, II, III, IV)
  • 2 Optional Subject Papers (Choose based on your strength)
  • Extensive answer writing practice is crucial
Step 4

Phase 3: Personality Test (Interview)

The final assessment of your administrative traits.

  • Interview by the UPSC board in New Delhi
  • Assessment of mental alertness, critical powers of assimilation, and leadership
Step 5

Academy Training

Preparation for active duty.

  • Foundation course at LBSNAA, Mussoorie
  • IAS officers continue at LBSNAA
  • IPS officers proceed to SVPNPA, Hyderabad for police training

Vocational & Skilled Trades

Carpenter

Master the art of woodworking, from framing houses to crafting fine furniture. A highly respected and essential trade.

Step 1

Basic Education & Foundation

Start with the basics of math and shop class.

  • High school diploma or GED
  • Take shop, geometry, and mechanical drawing classes
  • Familiarize yourself with basic hand tools
Step 2

Apprenticeship Program

The standard route to becoming a master tradesperson.

  • Join a 3-4 year carpentry apprenticeship
  • Complete required hours of paid on-the-job training
  • Complete required hours of technical classroom instruction
Step 3

Journeyman Carpenter

Work independently and hone your craft.

  • Pass your journey-level exams
  • Specialize in rough carpentry (framing) or finish carpentry (cabinetry)
  • Obtain necessary local licenses and insurance
Step 4

Master Carpenter / Contractor

Run your own business or lead major projects.

  • Earn a Master Carpenter designation through experience
  • Get a general contractor's license
  • Train your own apprentices

Painter & Decorator

Transform spaces with color and texture, working on residential, commercial, or industrial properties.

Step 1

Entry-Level Experience

Get your foot in the door.

  • No formal degree required (High school diploma preferred)
  • Work as a painter's helper or laborer
  • Learn surface prep (sanding, scraping, taping)
Step 2

Formal Apprenticeship

Learn the science of coatings and application.

  • Complete a 3-year apprenticeship program
  • Master brush, roller, and spray techniques
  • Learn to read blueprints and mix complex colors
Step 3

Journey-Level Painter

Execute complex jobs.

  • Specialize in faux finishes, wallpapering, or industrial coatings
  • Understand OSHA safety regulations (lead, asbestos)
  • Handle high-end residential or massive commercial contracts

Electrician

Keep the world powered up. One of the highest-paying and most technical skilled trades.

Step 1

Pre-Apprenticeship

Strong math skills are mandatory.

  • High school diploma with a focus on Algebra
  • Attend a trade school or pre-apprenticeship program (optional)
  • Understand basic electrical theory
Step 2

Electrical Apprenticeship

A rigorous combination of school and work.

  • Complete a 4-5 year apprenticeship (e.g., through JATC/IBEW)
  • Log 8,000+ hours of supervised on-the-job training
  • Master the National Electrical Code (NEC)
Step 3

Journeyman Electrician

Fully licensed to work independently.

  • Pass the state Journeyman Electrician exam
  • Specialize in residential, commercial, or industrial/lineman work
Step 4

Master Electrician

The highest level of certification.

  • Accumulate 4,000+ hours as a Journeyman
  • Pass the Master Electrician exam
  • Pull permits and start your own contracting business
