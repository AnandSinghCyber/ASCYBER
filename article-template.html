What Is the CIA Triad?

By Anand Singh August 19, 2026 5 min read

The CIA triad is an information security model that guides an organization's efforts and policies aimed at keeping its data secure. It stands for Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.

1. Confidentiality

Confidentiality is roughly equivalent to privacy. It is the component of the CIA triad that protects against unauthorized access to information. It ensures that data is only accessible by those who possess the proper authorization.

"In a world where data is the new oil, confidentiality is the vault that protects it."

Common Measures for Confidentiality:

  • Data encryption (at rest and in transit)
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA)
  • Biometric verification
  • Strong passwords and access control lists (ACLs)

2. Integrity

Integrity involves maintaining the consistency, accuracy, and trustworthiness of data over its entire life cycle. Data must not be changed in transit, and steps must be taken to ensure that data cannot be altered by unauthorized people (for example, in a breach of confidentiality).

// Example of an integrity check using a hashing algorithm
import hashlib
file_hash = hashlib.sha256(open('important_file.txt', 'rb').read()).hexdigest()
print(f"File SHA-256 Hash: {file_hash}")

3. Availability

Availability is best ensured by rigorously maintaining all hardware, performing hardware repairs immediately when needed, and maintaining a correctly functioning operating system environment that is free of software conflicts.

Conclusion

When you build a security program from the ground up, the CIA Triad provides a foundational checklist. Balancing these three pillars is the core challenge of modern cybersecurity.
